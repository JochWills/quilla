Step by step

  1. 1

    Capture the meeting

    Record the meeting in Quilla once your client agrees, upload the audio, import a Teams, Zoom or Google Meet transcript, or paste your own notes. Recordings are transcribed and then deleted, so only the text is kept.

  2. 2

    Draft all 13 sections

    Quilla drafts every section of a FAIS Record of Advice from the meeting, and shows the words each section came from. Anything that wasn't discussed is marked "not captured", never filled in.

  3. 3

    Check it before compliance does

    Missing fees, unconfirmed replacement costs, an unclear client decision: gaps are flagged beside the record. Fix them, or explain why they don't apply, and check again.

  4. 4

    Sign off and seal

    You confirm the record and sign it. Quilla seals a read-only copy with a digital fingerprint. If you change it later, your changes become a new version and the signed one stays exactly as it was.

  5. 5

    Export for the client file

    Download a PDF or Word document with your practice name, how each flagged item was resolved and the full history of the record.

What Quilla won't do. It never recommends products, judges suitability or adds facts that aren't in your notes. You review every record, and nothing is final until you sign it.
