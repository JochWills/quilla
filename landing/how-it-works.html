How it works

From a client meeting to a signed Record of Advice.

You hold the meeting. Quilla turns what was said into a FAIS Record of Advice, shows you what a compliance officer would query, and keeps a sealed copy of what you sign. Here's each step, as it looks in the app.

01 Capture

Start with your meeting notes.

Log the meeting from a client's page or the Meetings screen, and write up what happened, the way you already do.

  • Your notes come first. What the client told you, what you considered and recommended, the fees, and what they decided. Short notes work too.
  • Add a transcript if you have one. Upload it from Teams, Zoom or Google Meet (.vtt, .srt, .txt or .docx), or paste it in.
  • Quilla drafts from both, and uses only what's written there.

Transcripts can mishear names and amounts, so check those against your notes. Name the speakers once and the transcript reads naturally.

02 Draft

All 13 sections, drafted from what was said.

Quilla writes every section of a FAIS Record of Advice in formal, third-person wording: from the client's circumstances and needs to fees, replacement, conflicts and the client's decision.

  • See where it came from. Each section shows the words from your notes it's based on.
  • Nothing is filled in. If something wasn't discussed, it's marked "Not captured".
  • Your standard wording. Add your practice's fixed text, like your conflict of interest policy, once. It's attached to the right section of every draft.
  • Edit freely. Type straight into any section, or use Improve with AI to tidy the wording without adding anything.

03 Check

See what compliance would query, before they do.

Alongside the record, Quilla lists what's missing, unclear or unconfirmed, ranked critical, important or minor, and links each item to its section.

  • Fix it in the record, then mark it addressed with a short note.
  • Or mark it not applicable and say why. Your note goes into the record's history.
  • Check again after editing. Items you've resolved stay resolved.

04 Sign off

You sign. Quilla seals it.

Nothing is final until you sign. You confirm the declaration, the client's decision, and your name and FSP number.

  • Open critical items? You can still sign, but you give a written reason, and it's kept with the record.
  • Sealed on signing. Each sign-off saves a read-only version with a digital fingerprint (SHA-256) that shows if a copy has been altered.
  • Changes make a new version. Reopen the record to edit it. Version 1 stays exactly as you signed it.

05 Export

A clean record for the client file.

Download the record as a PDF, a Word document or plain text, on your own letterhead (logo, practice details and footer), with the client's decision, how each flagged item was resolved and the record's history.

  • Sealed versions carry an integrity block with the version, sealing time and fingerprint.
  • Drafts are watermarked DRAFT, so an unsigned record is never mistaken for a final one.
  • A full audit trail: drafting, edits, AI rewrites, resolved items, sign-off and exports are all logged, and never rewritten.

Your data

Download everything in your account, or one client's data, as a single file. Close your account whenever you like.

What Quilla will never do.

Give advice

It documents the advice you gave. It never recommends products or judges whether they suit the client.

Invent facts

Every draft comes from your notes. Anything missing is flagged for you, never filled in.

Sign for you

You review every record, and nothing is final until you sign it.

Where your data goes. Records are stored encrypted in Ireland and are private to your account. Drafting uses Claude, Anthropic's AI, and your notes aren't used to train it. Read the privacy policy
