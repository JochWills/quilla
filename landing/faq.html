Questions

What advisors ask us.

Does Quilla give financial advice?

No. It documents advice you've already given, using only your notes. It never suggests products or judges suitability, and anything missing from your notes is marked as not captured.

Does it replace my compliance officer?

No. Quilla flags common record-keeping gaps before your compliance officer sees the file, so fewer come back to you. They still review your records.

What does “Improve with AI” do?

It rewrites a section in clearer, more formal wording using only the facts already in that section and your notes. It never adds new facts, and you can undo it.

Which AI does Quilla use?

Quilla uses Claude, made by Anthropic. Your notes are sent to it only to draft, check and reword your records, and under Anthropic's commercial terms they aren't used to train its models. Quilla's own rules sit on top: it never adds facts that aren't in your notes, marks anything missing as not captured, and nothing is final until you sign.

What happens to my clients' information?

Records are private to your account and stored encrypted in Ireland. To draft a record, your notes are sent to Claude, Anthropic's AI, which doesn't use them for training. If you record a meeting, the audio is sent to Deepgram for transcription and deleted once the transcript is ready. Include only what the record needs, and leave out ID and account numbers. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I record meetings in Quilla?

Yes, once your client agrees to be recorded. Quilla asks you to confirm their consent first, transcribes the recording, then deletes the audio and keeps only the text. You can also upload a transcript from Teams, Zoom or Google Meet, or paste your own notes.

What if I disagree with a flagged item?

Mark it as not applicable and say why. Your reason is kept in the record's history and included in the export.

Can a signed record be changed?

Signing seals a read-only copy with a digital fingerprint. If you reopen a record to fix something, the sealed version stays exactly as it was and your changes become a new version when you sign again.

Can I make drafts sound like my practice?

Yes. In Templates you can add guidance for each section, like how you describe fees, and standard wording you use in every record, like your remuneration disclosure. Standard wording is added to the draft where you can see and edit it.

What formats can I export?

PDF and Word, with your practice name, your sign-off, how each flagged item was resolved and the record's full history. Drafts are clearly marked as not signed.
